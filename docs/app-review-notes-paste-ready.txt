SameQ is fully anonymous: no sign-up, no username or password. An anonymous account is created during onboarding, so there is no demo account. There is no chat, no DMs, no following, no search, no image uploads. Once a day everyone gets the same prompt, writes one short answer, and only then can read others' answers one at a time. Answers disappear after 24-36 hours.

== What changed in this build (1.0.4, build 11) ==
In the version currently on the App Store (1.0.3, build 8), Report and Block sat behind a small unlabeled "..." icon in the top right of the header. In this build they sit directly below every answer, with a flag icon and the visible text label "Report or block". The Terms of Use (Article 7) have also been rewritten to describe that new location, so the in-app text and the screen now match.

== How to reach the Report/Block controls ==
1. Complete onboarding (accept the Terms of Use with the checkbox, pick an emoji).
2. On the Today screen, type any text into the answer field and submit it.
3. Tap the button labelled "Read the world's answers". One answer shows at a time.
4. Directly below the answer, tap "Report or block" (flag icon + text label).
5. A sheet "About this reply" opens with "Report this reply" and "Block this person".

Other users' answers stay locked until you submit your own answer for the day. That is the core rule of the app, not a defect, so step 2 has to come first. The SameQ team posts answers in English every day, so there is always at least one answer to act on.

== Guideline 1.2 - all eight precautions ==
1. Age rating: set to 18+.
2. Terms agreement: onboarding requires an explicit checkbox before an account is created, with a one-line summary shown above it. The terms state near the top that we have zero tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users, review every report within 24 hours, and remove violating posts and suspend the accounts responsible. English and Japanese: Menu > Settings > Terms of Use.
3. Filtering: every answer and nickname is checked against a blocklist (English/Japanese) on the device and again on our server (a database trigger), so a modified client cannot bypass it. Verify by typing profanity into the answer field.
4. Flagging: "Report or block" below any answer > "Report this reply" > choose a reason > Submit. The answer disappears from your feed immediately.
5. Blocking: same sheet > "Block this person". Blocked people are listed under Menu > Settings, section "BLOCKED PEOPLE", where they can be unblocked.
6. Removing your own post: Today screen > below your own answer > "Delete this answer". The confirmation states you cannot answer the same prompt again; it leaves every feed immediately.
7. Acting within 24h: an answer reported by 3 different users is auto-hidden (1 report if the text matches our blocklist), and an author with 3 auto-hidden answers is auto-suspended. We also review the moderation queue manually at least daily (procedure: docs/moderation-runbook.md).
8. Contact: Menu > Settings > "Contact us" (first row) shows nakakei6439@gmail.com as copyable text, plus a button that opens a pre-filled email. The same contact appears on the suspended-account screen.
