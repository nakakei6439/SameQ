SameQ is a fully anonymous app: no sign-up form, no username/password. An anonymous account is created automatically during onboarding, so there is no demo account to provide. There is no chat, no DMs, no following, no search, no image uploads. Once a day everyone gets the same prompt, writes one short text answer, and only then can read other users' answers one at a time. Answers disappear after 24-36 hours.

How to reach the Report/Block controls:
1. Complete onboarding (accept the terms, pick an emoji).
2. On the Today screen, type any text and tap Submit.
3. Tap "Read answers from the world".
4. Tap "..." on any answer. Report and Block are both there. Other users' answers only unlock after you answer yourself - this is the app's core rule, not a bug. Operator-labeled answers ("SameQ運営") are always present, so this is verifiable even on a slow day.

== Guideline 1.2 - all eight precautions ==
1. Age rating: set to 18+.
2. Terms agreement: onboarding requires an explicit checkbox before an account is created. Terms state near the top that we have zero tolerance for objectionable content/abusive users, review reports within 24h, remove violating posts and suspend responsible accounts. A one-line summary is shown above the checkbox. Available in English and Japanese: Menu > Settings > Terms of Use.
3. Filtering: every answer and nickname is checked against a blocklist (JA/EN) on-device and again on our server (DB trigger), so a modified client can't bypass it. Try typing profanity into the answer field.
4. Flagging: "..." on any answer > Report > choose reason > Submit. Disappears from your feed immediately.
5. Blocking: same "..." menu > Block. Manage at Settings > Blocked users.
6. Removing your own post: Today screen > under your answer > "Delete this answer". Confirmation states you can't answer the same prompt again. Leaves everyone's feed immediately.
7. Acting within 24h: a post reported by 3 different users is auto-hidden (1 report if text matches our blocklist). An author with 3 auto-hidden posts is auto-suspended. We also manually review the moderation queue at least daily. Written procedure: docs/moderation-runbook.md in our repo.
8. Contact: Menu > Settings > Contact us (first row). Shows nakakei6439@gmail.com as selectable/copyable text, plus a button to open a prefilled email. Same contact on the account-suspended screen.
